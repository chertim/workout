Tempo Key (E – P – C – S)
E Eccentric  ·  P Pause bottom  ·  C Concentric  ·  S Pause top
e.g. 3-1-1-0 = 3 s lower · 1 s pause · 1 s lift · no pause at top
Program Principles
RIR targeting — Leave 1–2 reps in reserve per set. Proximity to failure drives hypertrophy, but RIR 0 every set increases injury risk and recovery cost (Schoenfeld, 2010).
Stretch-emphasis — Exercises are selected to maximise load at the lengthened position. Mechanotransduction is greatest when muscle is under tension at long lengths (Oranchuk et al., 2019; Maeo et al., 2021).
Postural balance — Pulling volume equals or exceeds pushing volume each week. Posterior chain work counters the anterior-dominant patterns of desk-based lifestyles.
CNS management — No conventional deadlifts or power cleans. RDLs and machine/cable variants achieve equivalent mechanical tension with significantly lower systemic fatigue.
Weekly volume — Chest ~8 sets · Back ~12 sets · Shoulders ~8 sets · Biceps ~4–6 · Triceps ~4–6 · Quads ~10 · Hamstrings ~6 · Adductors 2 · Abs 4. Appropriate for the set caps and 45-min constraint (Krieger, 2010; Schoenfeld et al., 2017).
Progressive Overload

Double progression: Once you complete all sets at the top of the rep range with RIR ≥ 2, increase load by the smallest available increment and reset to the bottom of the range. Microplates (0.5–1.25 kg) are strongly recommended for upper body. Re-evaluate exercise selection every 6–8 weeks.
